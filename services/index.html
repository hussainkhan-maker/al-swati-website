Stone cladding in Riyadh: systems, process & FAQ

Mechanical Fixing Systems
01

Up-Down Bracket System

A versatile angle-bracket fixing method that supports stone panels from above and below, distributing load evenly across the facade. Ideal for large-format natural stone and porcelain on high-rise and commercial buildings.

02

PEN Fixing System

A pin-and-groove mechanical fixing system that conceals all hardware behind the stone face, delivering a seamless, joint-free facade appearance. Preferred for premium residential and hospitality projects.

03

C-Channel Rail System

A continuous aluminium rail system that allows individual stone panels to be slid in and replaced without disturbing adjacent panels. Excellent for large commercial facades requiring future maintenance access.

Undercut Anchor Systems
01

Undercut Anchor Fixing

Think of this like a hidden bolt inside the stone itself. A small hole is drilled into the back of the stone panel and a stainless-steel anchor expands inside it. The anchor carries a hidden bracket that hangs on horizontal aluminium rails, fixed to vertical aluminium supports on the wall, so no screws or brackets are visible from the outside. We use Hilti-certified anchors for this system, which are tested to international safety standards and trusted on major construction projects worldwide. The result is a clean, seamless facade that looks like the stone is simply floating on the building.

02

Kerf Rail System

A narrow groove (kerf) is cut along the top and bottom edges of each stone panel, and the thin stainless-steel blade of a horizontal aluminium rail slides into it. The rails are fixed to vertical aluminium supports on wall brackets. We use Fischer-certified anchors and clips for this system, which are specially designed for challenging wall surfaces like hollow block and AAC where standard fixings may not hold. It's a simple, reliable method used for decades, especially for marble walls in building lobbies, hotel corridors, and feature walls where a tidy, hardware-free finish matters.
