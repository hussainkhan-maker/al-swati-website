White Riyadh stone and chiselled stone for facades in Irqa

White Riyadh stone is a local natural stone with a light, even colour. It suits modern commercial buildings and villas alike, and it is the stone we used on our project in Irqa.

  • Light colour: a light facade takes up less of the sun's heat than a dark one, and it keeps a clean, modern look.
  • Two finishes: smooth (honed) faces for fins, walls and frames; chiselled (manhoot) faces for bases, piers and feature walls at street level.
  • Natural character: every block is slightly different, which gives a stone facade its natural look.
  • Other materials: we also install beige and yellow limestone, granite, marble and porcelain. See marble installation in Riyadh for entrances and feature walls.

What to check in a sample: look at several pieces side by side for colour and veining, check the finish (smooth or chiselled) on a real piece, ask how corners and fin returns will be finished, and compare the sample with the stone that arrives on site.

Facade standards for buildings in Irqa

  • Codes and materials: the facade design follows the Saudi Building Code (SBC), and all our materials comply with SASO specifications.
  • Engineer's approval: the fixing design is approved by the project's engineer before work starts.
  • Wind and height: brackets, anchors and their spacing are chosen for the panel size, the height of the facade and its exposure.
  • Stainless steel fixings: every panel is carried by fixings anchored into the structure, never by adhesive alone, including tall fins and panels above shop fronts.
  • Movement joints: joints let the facade move with heat without cracking.
  • Certified systems: we fix with Hilti and Fischer certified systems: Up-Down brackets, PEN pins, C-Channel rails and undercut anchors, chosen to suit each building.

How our facades handle Riyadh's 45°C summer heat and dust

Summer in Riyadh reaches about 45°C, and dust storms are part of the year. A mechanically fixed stone facade is built for that.

  • Thermal movement: stone and walls expand in the heat and contract at night; movement joints and adjustable brackets take that movement.
  • Ventilated gap: mechanical fixing leaves a gap behind the panels, so air moves behind them, heat stays off the wall and moisture dries out.
  • Dust: light honed stone and chiselled stone show dust less than dark polished stone.
  • Cleaning after sandstorms: use water and a mild cleaner with a soft brush. Never use acid on limestone or marble.
  • Sealing: porous natural stone benefits from a breathable sealer.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work in Irqa?

Yes. In 2025 we completed two commercial buildings in Irqa: white Riyadh stone and chiselled stone on one, and white Riyadh stone frames around glass curtain walls on the other. We also work on villas and other buildings across Riyadh.

Smooth or chiselled stone for my building?

Smooth (honed) white stone gives clean, modern lines on fins, walls and frames. Chiselled stone adds texture and hides dust and small marks, so it suits bases, piers and feature walls at street level. Many buildings use both, like our project in Irqa.

Can stone be used on tall fins and above shop fronts?

Yes, with mechanical fixing. Each panel is carried by fixings anchored into the structure, and the fixing design is approved by the project's engineer.

Do you supply the material too?

Yes. We supply and install natural stone, marble, granite and porcelain cladding for facades and walls. We do not do flooring.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. Every contract includes a written workmanship warranty of at least 5 years.
