AL-Swati Contracting Privacy Notice

Who We Are

AL-Swati Contracting ("we", "us") is a construction and stone installation contractor based in Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. This notice explains how we handle personal information when you use our website al-swaticontracting.com.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account. We only receive the information you choose to send us — for example your name, phone number, email address and project details when you contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, or when you use the quote form (which opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message on your own device).

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations, deliver and follow up on our services, and meet our legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us, and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your Rights
