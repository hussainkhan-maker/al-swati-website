AL-Swati Contracting Privacy Notice

Who We Are

AL-Swati Contracting ("we", "us") is a construction and stone installation contractor based in Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. This notice explains how we handle personal information when you use our website al-swaticontracting.com.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account. We only receive the information you choose to send us — for example your name, phone number, email address and project details when you contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, or when you send one of the forms on this website.

The forms on this website (quote request, technical request, send drawings and the cost calculator) send what you type to our enquiry system so that we can reply: your name, company, phone number, email address, project details, any link you paste and your message. With it we record the page the form was on, its language, the website you came from and any campaign tag in the link. The enquiry system is hosted by a cloud service provider. To stop automated abuse, it keeps a scrambled fingerprint of the network address that changes every day, for each form sent and each press of the WhatsApp, call or email buttons; it does not keep the address itself. The quote form and the calculator can also open a WhatsApp message on your own device; that message is sent only if you send it.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and performance purposes.

How We Use It

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations, deliver and follow up on our services, and meet our legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-Party Services

Our website uses third-party services including Google Maps (embedded map), Google Fonts and WhatsApp. When you interact with these services, their own privacy policies apply. Links to other websites are provided for convenience; we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Counting Visits

To learn which pages and videos help our clients, the website counts visits with its own simple counter. For each page it records the page address, the kind of site you came from (for example Google, TikTok or YouTube), whether you used a phone, and whether you pressed the WhatsApp, call, email or quote button. The counter does not use cookies, and it does not save your IP address, your name or your number. What you type is saved only when you send a form, as described above. Your browser keeps where the visit started only until you close the tab.

Cookies

Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Embedded third-party services may set their own cookies. You can manage cookies in your browser settings.

Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us, and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us at info@al-swaticontracting.com.

Children

Our services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes & Contact

We may update this notice from time to time; the latest version will always be posted on this page. Questions can be sent to info@al-swaticontracting.com or by phone on +966 51 182 6313.
