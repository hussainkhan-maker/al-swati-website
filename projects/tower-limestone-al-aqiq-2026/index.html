AL-Swati Contracting is supplying and installing the limestone facade of a tower in Al Aqiq, Riyadh. Our scope covers both the stone and its installation: about 5,000 m² of limestone cladding, fixed mechanically to the structure with stainless steel Up-Down brackets. The work is in progress in 2026, and our own team is on site.

Al Aqiq is a district in the north of Riyadh with many towers and commercial buildings. A tower facade there has to look clean and even across many floors, and it has to stay safe and straight for decades in Riyadh's sun and dust. This page shows the work as it stands, with photos from the site. You can see more of our work on our projects page.

What a tower facade like this needs

A stone facade on a tower is a different job from a villa. The panels sit high above the street, so every panel must be carried by the building's structure, not held by adhesive or mortar alone. The fixing has to take the weight of the stone and the wind, and it has to let the panels move a little as the building and the stone heat up in the day and cool down at night.

The facade also has to read as one surface. On a tower, small differences in colour, joint width or alignment repeat floor after floor until they show from the street. That calls for careful setting out, consistent stone, and a team that keeps the same standard from the first row to the last. The work itself is done at height, from safety scaffolding, which needs planning and discipline every day.

The solution: limestone on Up-Down brackets

Limestone gives the tower a warm, natural colour that suits Riyadh's architecture and looks calm in strong sunlight. Because it is a natural stone, each panel varies a little in tone and texture, which gives a large facade depth instead of a flat, printed look.

The panels are fixed with the Up-Down bracket system. Stainless steel angle brackets are anchored into the structural wall with Hilti and Fischer certified anchors, and each bracket supports the stone panels from above and below, so the load is spread evenly across the facade. Every panel is carried on its own brackets, and the brackets are adjusted during installation so the joints stay straight and even. With mechanical fixing, our standard thickness for natural stone is 3 cm.

Our wider stone cladding work in Riyadh uses the same certified fixing systems (Up-Down brackets, PEN pins, C-Channel rails and undercut anchors), chosen to suit each building.

How we install a facade like this

Our own direct workforce installs every facade; we do not subcontract. On a project like this the work follows the same steps:

  1. Site survey and measurement of the structure, openings and edges.
  2. Fixing design, approved by the project's engineer before work starts.
  3. Setting out of lines and levels, so rows and joints run true across the whole facade.
  4. Anchors and brackets: Hilti or Fischer anchors into the structure, then the stainless steel Up-Down brackets set and levelled.
  5. Stone panels and movement joints: each panel is hung on its brackets, with movement joints where the design needs them.
  6. Cleaning and inspection of every finished area.
  7. Handover with our written workmanship warranty.

Built for Riyadh's heat and dust

A mechanically fixed facade leaves a gap between the stone and the wall. Air can move behind the panels, which helps keep heat off the wall behind and lets moisture dry out. Movement joints let the stone expand and contract in Riyadh's strong sun without cracking, and stainless steel fixings do not rust behind the stone. The warm colour of limestone also hides fine dust better than dark, polished surfaces.

Frequently asked questions

Can limestone be used on a high-rise tower in Riyadh?

Yes, when it is fixed mechanically. On this tower each limestone panel is carried by stainless steel Up-Down brackets anchored into the structure with Hilti and Fischer anchors, so the stone does not depend on adhesive to stay in place.

What does the Up-Down bracket system do?

Stainless steel angle brackets support each stone panel from above and below and spread the load evenly across the facade. The brackets are adjusted during installation, which keeps the joints straight across large areas.

Do you supply the stone as well as install it?

Yes. On this tower our scope is the supply and installation of the limestone. We supply and install natural stone, marble, granite and porcelain cladding for facades and walls, all with mechanical fixing.
