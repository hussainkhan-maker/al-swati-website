What Our Riyadh Projects Have in Common

Every project in this portfolio was built by our own team, with no subcontracting. Our team brings 500+ projects of experience since 1994 in stone, marble and porcelain across Riyadh, from villas in Al Nakheel to commercial buildings in Irqa, a tower in Al Aqiq and a mall in Aziziya. The building types change; the way we work does not.

Residential Villas

Villa facades in Riyadh stone, limestone, sandstone and granite, with polished marble cladding at entrances and on feature walls. See our stone cladding and marble installation services.

Commercial Towers and Malls

Large-format porcelain and natural stone facades for commercial buildings, towers and malls, fixed mechanically so every panel stays secure and straight in Riyadh's heat and dust.

Government and Institutional Buildings

Projects with strict specifications. We work to the approved shop drawings and submit materials and samples for the consultant's approval.

How We Deliver Every Project

  1. Free quote. Tell us about your building. We will reply as soon as possible.
  2. Material selection. We help you choose the stone, marble or porcelain that suits the building, Riyadh's climate and your budget.
  3. Mechanical fixing. Anchor bolts into the structural wall, levelled C-channels, and Up-Down brackets (stainless or hot-dip galvanised steel, per the project specification) set into a groove in each stone panel.
  4. Handover with warranty. Our own team completes the work, with a 5-year written workmanship warranty attested by the Chamber of Commerce, issued by the contracting company.
