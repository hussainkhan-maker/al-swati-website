How the timeline is worked out

Material delivery comes first: about 2 weeks for Riyadh white and beige stone and 4 weeks for granite, travertine and porcelain, plus 1 week to put up safety scaffolding on a tower. Then one team of a mason and a helper installs 6–8 m² a day; we add a team for every 100 m², up to 4 teams on one project, so 1,000 m² takes about 5–7 weeks of installation.

FAQ

Stone Cladding Cost Questions

How much does stone cladding cost per m² in Riyadh?

On Up-Down brackets, supply and installation in Riyadh costs about 230–280 SAR per m² for Riyadh white stone, 290–375 for Riyadh beige or yellow limestone, 265–340 for granite, 505–700 for travertine marble and 265–340 for large-format porcelain, excluding VAT.

How much does a villa facade cost?

Multiply the facade area by the price per m². For example, 500 m² of Riyadh white stone on Up-Down brackets comes to about SAR 115,000–140,000 excluding VAT. Use the calculator above for your own area and material.

Do C-Channel, kerf or undercut systems cost more?

Yes. They take more installation work and more fixing hardware than Up-Down brackets. For Riyadh white stone, for example, the price goes from about 230–280 SAR per m² with Up-Down to 350–410 with C-Channel rails, 485–545 with kerf and 530–590 with undercut anchors. The table above has every material.

How long does stone cladding installation take?

Material delivery takes about 2 weeks for Riyadh white and beige stone and 4 weeks for granite, travertine and porcelain. Then one team of a mason and a helper installs 6–8 m² a day; we add a team for every 100 m², up to 4 teams on one project, so 1,000 m² takes about 5–7 weeks of installation. A tower needs about 1 more week to put up safety scaffolding.

Is VAT included in these prices?

No. All prices on this page exclude 15% VAT, which is added in the quote.

Is the calculator's price final?

No. It is an estimate from our current price list. The exact price depends on your drawings, the stone you choose, panel sizes, height and access, so we confirm it after we see the drawings or visit the site. Shop drawings, when your consultant needs them, are quoted separately. Send the estimate on WhatsApp and we reply within one business day.

Why mechanical fixing instead of cement?

Mechanically fixed stone hangs on stainless-steel brackets, rails or anchors, so it does not depend on cement that can crack in Riyadh's 45°C heat. We use certified Hilti and Fischer systems and give a minimum 5-year written workmanship warranty.
