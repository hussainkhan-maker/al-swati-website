How the timeline is worked out

Material delivery comes first: about 2 weeks for Riyadh stone and 4 weeks for imported materials, plus 1 week to set up a gondola or scaffolding on a tower. One crew then installs about 25 m² a day, and we add a crew for every 250 m², up to 4 crews on one project.

FAQ

Stone Cladding Cost Questions

How much does stone cladding cost per m² in Riyadh?

With supply and installation using mechanical fixing, our 2026 prices in Riyadh are 250–350 SAR per m² for Riyadh natural stone, 350–550 for limestone, 300–500 for granite, 500–700 for polished marble and 250–400 for large-format porcelain.

How much does a villa facade cost?

Multiply the facade area by the price per m². For example, 500 m² of Riyadh natural stone with Up-Down brackets comes to about SAR 125,000–175,000. Use the calculator above for your own area and material.

Do C-Channel rails or undercut anchors cost more?

Yes. Up-Down brackets are included in the prices above. A vertical C-Channel rail system adds about 50 SAR per m², and undercut anchors about 100 SAR per m².

How long does stone cladding installation take?

Material delivery takes about 2 weeks for Riyadh stone and 4 weeks for imported materials. After that one crew installs about 25 m² a day and we put up to 4 crews on a project, so 1,000 m² takes about 2 weeks of installation.

Is the calculator's price final?

No. It is an estimate from our current price list. The exact price depends on your drawings, the stone you choose, panel sizes, height and access, so we confirm it after we see the drawings or visit the site. Send the estimate on WhatsApp and we reply within one business day.

Why mechanical fixing instead of cement?

Mechanically fixed stone hangs on stainless-steel brackets, rails or anchors, so it does not depend on cement that can crack in Riyadh's 45°C heat. We use certified Hilti and Fischer systems and give a minimum 5-year written workmanship warranty.
