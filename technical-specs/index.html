Mechanical Fixing vs Wet Cement Fixing

How the two methods hold up in Riyadh. Select a row to see why.

Mechanical fixingWet cement fixing
Mechanical fixing

Each panel is held by mechanical fixings with a joint around it, so it expands and contracts freely with the daily heat.

Wet cement fixing

Stone, mortar and wall expand at different rates. Day-and-night heat cycles stress the mortar bond and can open hollow spots behind the stone.

Mechanical fixing

The project engineer calculates the wind load for each project to the current SBC 301 and sets the fixing type and spacing for it.

Wet cement fixing

Holding power depends on full mortar contact behind every panel, which cannot be checked once the stone is set.

Mechanical fixing

No mortar behind the stone, so no cement salts wash out onto the face: no mortar efflorescence.

Wet cement fixing

Water moving through cement mortar carries salts to the face and leaves white efflorescence stains, most visible on light Riyadh limestone.

Mechanical fixing

Every panel is held by mechanical fixings, so none can fall from a failed mortar bond.

Wet cement fixing

If the mortar bond fails behind a panel, nothing else holds it, and hollow spots are not visible from outside.

Mechanical fixing

A damaged panel can be released without breaking out mortar; with undercut anchors on rails each panel is replaced on its own.

Wet cement fixing

Replacing a panel means chiselling out mortar, which risks cracking the panels around it.

FAQ

Questions from Consultants

What stone thickness do you use on mechanically fixed facades?

Natural stone is typically 30 mm, thicker on demand. Marble and granite are often 20 mm; exterior 20 mm panels on edge fixings need the engineer's check and stone tests, or an undercut system. Porcelain thickness follows the panel maker's system.

How is wind load designed?

The project engineer calculates the wind load for each project to the current Saudi Building Code (SBC 301), and that sets the fixing type and spacing.

Which fixing systems do you install?

Up-Down angle brackets, PEN pin fixing, vertical C-Channel rails, kerf rails and undercut anchors. We can install systems from Hilti, fischer and Rockany, and local or imported accessories.

What do you submit for approval?

Material submittals and samples before work starts. A mock-up and anchor pull-out tests are arranged on request, and installation follows the approved shop drawings.

Can we get a method statement and reference drawings for our project?

Yes. Send the request form on this page with your company and contact details. We will reply as soon as possible.
