Mechanically Fixed Marble Cladding

AL-Swati Contracting supplies and installs marble cladding for luxury villas, commercial buildings, mosques and government facilities across Riyadh and Saudi Arabia.

We install marble only with mechanical fixing. Each panel is kerfed or drilled at its edges, then hung on Up-Down angles or PEN pins over a C-Channel rail system. Heavy panels stay safe on high walls and in Riyadh heat, with no risk of them coming loose as adhesive ages.

Methods

How We Fix Marble

01

Up-Down Bracket System

Up-Down angles locate in kerfs cut into the top and bottom edges of each marble panel, so every panel is carried on its own.

02

PEN Fixing System

Pins sit in holes drilled into the panel edges, completely hidden behind the marble face for a clean finish.

03

C-Channel Rail System

Vertical C-Channels are anchored to the wall and carry the Up-Down angles, to the engineer's design, so marble panels sit plumb and level.

Marble Facades

Mechanical Fixing of Marble on Facades

On an outside wall, marble has to carry its own weight and stand up to sun and wind for many years. That is why no panel on our facades depends on cement or adhesive.

01

Each panel carried on its own

Metal fixings sit in the edges of every marble panel and take its weight back to the wall. No panel rests on the one below it.

02

An air gap behind the marble

The panels stand off the wall, which helps thermal insulation and keeps damp in the wall from reaching the face of the marble.

03

Fixings to the engineer's calculation

The anchors, the rails and the spacing of the fixings follow the calculation of the project's facade engineer for the height and the panel size.

Cost

What Decides the Cost of Marble Installation in Riyadh

There is no single price per square metre for marble. These six things move the price of a job up or down.

01

The marble itself

The type and origin of the marble, its colour and veining. The stone is usually the largest part of the cost.

02

Panel size and thickness

Large or thick panels cost more to buy, are heavier to lift and need stronger fixings.

03

Area and height

A larger area lowers the cost per square metre. Height adds scaffolding, lifting and time.

04

The fixing system

Up-Down angles fixed straight to a sound wall cost less than a full C-Channel frame, which is needed when the wall is uneven or a wider gap is wanted.

05

The wall behind

Concrete, block or steel, and how straight the wall is, decide the anchors and how much frame is needed.

06

Details and openings

Windows, corners, columns, curves and cut-outs take more cutting and more labour than a flat wall.

Common Questions

Marble Installation FAQ

How much does marble installation cost in Riyadh?

It depends on the type of marble, the panel size and thickness, the area and height of the wall, the fixing system and the amount of detail around openings and corners. Our online calculator gives a price range for a facade, and after a free site visit we send a written quotation.

Do you supply the marble or only install it?

We do both. We can supply premium marble and granite, or install panels you have already chosen. Either way, every panel is fixed mechanically.

Why do you install marble only with mechanical fixing?

Marble panels are heavy, and adhesive or mortar on its own can weaken with Riyadh heat and time. Mechanical fixing carries each panel on metal fixings, so it stays safely fixed on facades and interior walls.

Can you fix large marble slabs on high walls?

Yes. Large slabs are fixed with Up-Down angles or PEN pins on C-Channel rails, so each panel is carried on its own and can be replaced later without disturbing its neighbours.

Which areas do you work in?

We are based in Riyadh and work on projects across the Riyadh region and the rest of Saudi Arabia.
