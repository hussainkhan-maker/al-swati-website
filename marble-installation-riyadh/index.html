Mechanically Fixed Marble Cladding

AL-Swati Contracting supplies and installs marble cladding for luxury villas, commercial buildings, mosques and government facilities across Riyadh and Saudi Arabia.

We install marble only with mechanical fixing. Each panel is kerfed or drilled at its edges, then hung on Up-Down angles or PEN pins over a C-Channel rail system. Heavy panels stay safe on high walls and in Riyadh heat, with no risk of them coming loose as adhesive ages.

Methods

How We Fix Marble

01

Up-Down Bracket System

Stainless-steel Up-Down angles locate in kerfs cut into the top and bottom edges of each marble panel, so every panel is carried on its own.

02

PEN Fixing System

Stainless-steel pins sit in holes drilled into the panel edges, completely hidden behind the marble face for a clean finish.

03

C-Channel Rail System

Vertical aluminium C-Channels are anchored to the wall and carry the Up-Down angles, so marble panels sit plumb and level and any single panel can be replaced later.

Common Questions

Marble Installation FAQ

Do you supply the marble or only install it?

We do both. We can supply premium marble and granite, or install panels you have already chosen. Either way, every panel is fixed mechanically.

Why do you install marble only with mechanical fixing?

Marble panels are heavy, and adhesive or mortar on its own can weaken with Riyadh heat and time. Mechanical fixing carries each panel on stainless steel, so it stays safe for decades on facades and interior walls.

Can you fix large marble slabs on high walls?

Yes. Large slabs are fixed with Up-Down angles or PEN pins on C-Channel rails, so each panel is carried on its own and can be replaced later without disturbing its neighbours.

Which areas do you work in?

We are based in Riyadh and work on projects across the Riyadh region and the rest of Saudi Arabia.
