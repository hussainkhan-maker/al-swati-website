Our Story

Built on Craftsmanship

The AL-Swati Contracting team's experience in Riyadh goes back to 1994, with a single focus: delivering precision stone installation work that stands the test of time. That focus has never wavered.

What began as a specialist mechanical installation team has grown into one of Riyadh’s dedicated stone contractors, focused entirely on natural stone, marble and porcelain cladding, installed with mechanical fixing.

Today we operate across Riyadh's most prestigious residential, commercial, and institutional projects, trusted by developers, architects, and private clients who demand the highest standard of finish.

Our Methods

Tools & Fixing Methods

We invest in the best tools so our clients get the best results.

✓Undercut Anchor SystemsUndercut anchors for concealed fixing. Our experience includes Hilti undercut anchors on 30 mm natural stone and on 20 mm porcelain.
✓Pin (PEN) and Kerf Rail FixingPin and kerf rail fixing in the panel edges for natural stone, to the engineer's design.
✓C-Channel Rail SystemsVertical channels that carry the stone brackets, to the engineer's design.
✓Mechanical Updown FixingUp-Down brackets in stainless or hot-dip galvanised steel, according to the project specification.
