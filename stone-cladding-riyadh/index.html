Our Fixing Systems

Mechanical Stone Cladding Systems

The fixing system matters as much as the stone. These are the systems we install on Riyadh facades.

01

Up-Down Bracket System

Angle brackets support each stone panel from above and below and spread the load evenly. Ideal for large natural stone and porcelain panels on villas and commercial buildings.

02

PEN Fixing System

A pin-and-groove system that hides all hardware behind the stone face for a clean, joint-free look. Popular for premium villa facades.

03

C-Channel Rail System

Vertical channels on wall brackets carry the stone brackets. Suited to large commercial facades and tall storeys.

Common Questions

Stone Cladding FAQ

How much does stone cladding cost in Riyadh?

The cost depends on the stone type, panel size and thickness, the fixing system and the size of the facade. Natural stone such as granite or premium limestone costs more than large-format porcelain. We visit the site, measure the facade and give you a clear, detailed quote free of charge.

Which fixing system is best for a villa facade?

For most villa facades we recommend a mechanical fixing system rather than adhesive alone. Up-Down brackets and PEN fixing suit residential facades, while undercut anchors give a fully concealed finish. We choose the system based on the stone, the wall and the height of the building.

Is natural stone or porcelain better for the Riyadh climate?

Both perform well when installed correctly. Dense natural stones handle 45°C summer heat very well, while porcelain has very low water absorption and keeps its colour. The right choice depends on the look you want and your budget, and we will advise you honestly.

How long does a stone cladding project take?

An average villa facade takes about two to four weeks. Large commercial facades can take several months. We provide a detailed timeline with every quote.

Do you give a warranty on stone cladding?

Yes. Every installation carries a 5-year written workmanship warranty attested by the Chamber of Commerce, issued by the contracting company, and all work is carried out by our own installation team.
