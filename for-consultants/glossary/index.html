Arabic–English Glossary

The words used on site in Riyadh and the words used in specifications are not always the same. This glossary pairs them, so drawings, submittals and site instructions mean the same thing to everyone.

Terms

Fixing, Testing and Submittal Terms

EnglishArabicMeaning
Mechanical fixingالتثبيت الميكانيكيHolding each panel on metal fixings instead of bedding it in cement mortar.
Stone cladding (installation)تركيب الحجرFixing stone panels to a facade or wall. In Saudi usage the work is called تركيب.
Riyadh limestoneحجر الرياض (حجر جيري)A trade name for limestone from several quarries around Riyadh; properties come from tests on the actual source.
PanelلوحOne piece of stone or porcelain as fixed on the wall.
ThicknessالسماكةPanel thickness, set by the fixing type and the tested strength of the stone.
Substructureالهيكل المعدني (الشاسيه)The steel or aluminium frame between the building and the stone fixings.
Bracket / angleزاوية تثبيتThe metal part that connects the panel fixing to the wall or to the substructure.
Up-Down angle (split-tail anchor)زاوية Up-DownAn angle with one tongue up into the panel above and one down into the panel below.
Pin / dowelدبوس (PEN)A round pin in holes drilled in the panel edges.
Kerfشق الحافة (Kerf)A slot sawn along the panel edge to receive a blade or tongue.
Kerf railمجرى KerfA rail whose blade sits in the kerf, locally or continuously.
C-Channelمجرى C-ChannelA vertical steel channel that carries the stone brackets and spans between strong points.
Undercut anchorمرساة UndercutAn anchor locked by shape into a widened hole in the back of the panel.
Hanger (agraffe)مشبك التعليقThe clasp on an undercut anchor that hooks onto the horizontal rail.
Loadbearing fixingتثبيت حاملA fixing that carries the weight of the panel.
Restraint fixingتثبيت ساندA fixing that holds the panel against wind but carries no weight.
Wall anchorانكرThe anchor that fixes a bracket to concrete or masonry.
Pull-out testاختبار السحبA site test that loads an installed anchor to a proof load.
Mock-upنموذج تجريبي (Mock-up)A sample area built before the work, approved as the benchmark.
Shop drawingsالمخططات التنفيذيةDrawings that show every panel, fixing and detail as it will be built.
Material submittalطلب اعتماد موادData and samples submitted for the consultant's approval.
Method statementطريقة التنفيذThe written sequence and controls for the work.
Inspection and test plan (ITP)خطة الفحص والاختبارThe list of checks, criteria, records and hold points.
Hold pointنقطة توقفA stage where work stops until the consultant releases it.
Witness pointنقطة حضورA stage the consultant is invited to witness.
Non-conformance report (NCR)تقرير عدم مطابقةThe record of work that does not match the approved documents.
Movement jointفاصل حركةA joint that lets the facade move with temperature and the structure.
Open jointفاصل مفتوحA joint left unsealed in a ventilated facade.
Ventilated cavityفراغ مهوّىThe air space behind the stone.
Fixed point / sliding pointنقطة ثابتة / نقطة منزلقةWhere a member is held, and where it is free to move with temperature.
Shimsرقائق ضبطThin packers used to adjust a bracket; stainless steel or plastic.
Isolatorعازل بين المعادنA washer or pad that separates two different metals.
Stainless steelستانلس ستيلCorrosion-resistant steel used for fixings.
Hot-dip galvanisedمجلفن على الساخنSteel coated with zinc by dipping after fabrication.
Wind loadأحمال الرياحPressure and suction from wind, calculated for each project to SBC 301.
Safety factorمعامل الأمانThe factor applied to tested strength to obtain an allowable value.
Flexural strengthمقاومة الانحناءThe bending strength of the stone, from tests.
Lippageفرق المنسوب بين لوحينThe step between the faces of two adjacent panels.
Toleranceالتفاوت المسموحThe permitted deviation in line, level, plumb and joint width.
EfflorescenceالتزهّرWhite salt deposits on the face, usually from cement mortar and moisture.
Back meshشبكة خلفيةMesh bonded to the back of a panel to hold fragments if it breaks.
European Technical Assessment (ETA)التقييم الفني الأوروبيA published assessment of a construction product, such as an undercut anchor.
Workmanship warrantyضمان التنفيذThe installer's written warranty on the installation work.
Note

How We Use These Words

System names such as Up-Down, PEN, C-Channel, Kerf and Undercut are used in Latin letters on Riyadh sites and in our documents, in both languages. Where a project specification defines a term differently, the specification governs.
