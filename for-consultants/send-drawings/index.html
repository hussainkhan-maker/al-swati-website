Tell Us About the Project

What the reply contains

  • The fixing systems that suit your stone, wall and height.
  • What published guidance says about thickness for your stone and fixing type.
  • Whether the wall is likely to need a substructure, for the engineer to confirm.
  • A list of the tests to ask for.

The reply is indicative guidance from an installer. It is not a design, a calculation or a quotation; the project is designed by its engineer or consultant.

Useful to include

What to Send

  • Elevations and typical wall sections, as PDF or DWG.
  • Building height and storey heights.
  • The stone or panel type, the finish and the panel sizes you would like.
  • The backup wall: reinforced concrete, solid block or hollow block.
  • The specification section for the stone, if it exists, and the stage of the project.
