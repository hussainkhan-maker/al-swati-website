Porcelain for villa facades in Al Nakheel

Large-format porcelain suits the modern villas being built in Al Nakheel. It can look like travertine, marble or natural stone, with an even colour from panel to panel, so frames, columns and parapets look clean and consistent.

  • Low water absorption: porcelain is dense, so it does not need sealing and keeps its look.
  • Colour in strong sun: the colour is fired into the panel and stays stable in Riyadh's sun.
  • Large panels, fewer joints: big formats give long, clean lines on modern designs.
  • Other materials: we also install natural Riyadh stone, limestone, granite and marble. See marble installation in Riyadh for entrances and feature walls.

What to check in a sample: look at several panels side by side for colour and pattern, check the edges and corners, ask to see how a corner or soffit will be finished, and compare the sample with the panels that arrive on site.

Facade standards for villas in Al Nakheel

  • Codes and materials: the facade design follows the Saudi Building Code (SBC), and all our materials comply with SASO specifications.
  • Engineer's approval: the fixing design is approved by the project's engineer before work starts.
  • Wind and height: brackets, anchors and their spacing are chosen for the panel size, the height of the facade and its exposure.
  • Stainless steel fixings: every panel is carried by fixings anchored into the structure, never by adhesive alone, including panels under soffits and cantilevers.
  • Movement joints: joints let the facade move with heat without cracking.
  • Certified systems: we fix with Hilti and Fischer certified systems: Up-Down brackets, PEN pins, C-Channel rails and undercut anchors, chosen to suit each building.

How our facades handle Riyadh's 45°C summer heat and dust

Summer in Riyadh reaches about 45°C, and dust storms are part of the year. A mechanically fixed facade is built for that.

  • Thermal movement: panels and walls expand in the heat and contract at night; movement joints and adjustable brackets take that movement.
  • Ventilated gap: mechanical fixing leaves a gap behind the panels, so air moves behind them, heat stays off the wall and moisture dries out.
  • Dust: porcelain's dense surface does not soak up dust; natural stone in honed or textured finishes shows dust less than dark polished stone.
  • Cleaning after sandstorms: use water and a mild cleaner with a soft brush. Never use acid on marble or limestone.
  • Sealing: porous natural stone benefits from a breathable sealer; porcelain does not need one.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work on villas in Al Nakheel?

Yes. We are installing about 3,000 m² of porcelain on a group of new villas in Al Nakheel right now, and we work on villas and other buildings across Riyadh.

Porcelain or natural stone for my villa?

Porcelain gives an even colour and very low water absorption, which suits modern designs. Natural stone gives a natural, varied look that suits classic and Najdi-style villas. We can show samples of both.

Can porcelain be used on soffits and cantilevered parts?

Yes, with mechanical fixing. Each panel is carried by fixings anchored into the structure, so panels overhead do not depend on adhesive.

Do you supply the material too?

Yes. We supply and install natural stone, marble, granite and porcelain cladding for facades and walls. We do not do flooring.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. Every contract includes a written workmanship warranty of at least 5 years.
