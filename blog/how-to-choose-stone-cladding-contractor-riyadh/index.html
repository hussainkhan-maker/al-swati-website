How to Choose a Reliable Stone Cladding Contractor in Riyadh

A complete guide for villa owners and property developers in Riyadh on how to vet a professional stone contractor — before you sign anything.

Choosing a stone cladding contractor in Riyadh is not a decision to rush. The facade is the first thing anyone sees, and the last thing you want to fix a year after completion. This guide walks you through exactly how to verify a contractor's competence before you sign the contract.

1. Check Their Track Record

Ask the contractor for photos of completed projects in Riyadh specifically — not just a general portfolio. More importantly, request a site visit to one of their finished projects so you can see the quality of workmanship with your own eyes. Look for:

  • Even, consistent joints between stones
  • No cracking or separation from the wall surface
  • Clean, precise corner and edge treatment
  • How the facade has aged over time

2. Ask About the Fixing Systems They Use

A professional contractor knows the difference between fixing systems and selects the right one for each project. The main systems used in the Saudi market:

  • Up-Down Bracket system: Suited for heavy facades, provides flexibility during installation
  • PEN Fixing system: Precise and concealed, ideal for thin stone panels
  • C-Channel Rail system: The strongest option for commercial and institutional projects
  • Hilti and Fischer undercut anchors: For mechanical fixing drilled directly beneath the stone surface

If a contractor cannot explain these systems clearly, that is a serious warning sign.

3. Verify Compliance with Saudi Quality Standards

In Saudi Arabia, facade work is subject to requirements set by the Saudi Contractors Authority (SCA). Make sure your contractor:

  • Is registered with the Saudi Contractors Authority
  • Uses materials that comply with Saudi standards (SASO)
  • Provides a minimum 5-year workmanship warranty

4. The Right Questions to Ask Before Signing

Before any contract is signed, ask these questions directly:

  1. Who actually performs the installation — your direct workforce or subcontractors?
  2. What stone type do you recommend for Riyadh's climate?
  3. How do you handle thermal expansion during summer?
  4. What exactly does the warranty cover?
  5. Do you offer scheduled maintenance after handover?
